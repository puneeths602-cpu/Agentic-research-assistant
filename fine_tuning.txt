Fine-tuning adapts a pre-trained language model to a domain or task. Full fine-tuning updates all weights, while parameter-efficient methods such as LoRA train small adapter matrices and need far less memory.
Prompt engineering and RAG are cheaper than fine-tuning and should be tried first. Fine-tuning is useful for consistent style, structured output and domain vocabulary.
Typical NLP tasks for adapted models are summarisation, text classification and text generation. These are evaluated with ROUGE, accuracy and F1 respectively.
